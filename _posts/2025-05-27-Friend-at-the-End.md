In a dream, I saw a light along the hill
pulling me into my final moments.

But then, from a nearby room, a voice
called out to me. The door was ajar
and you offered me a chair. There,
We sat and reveled in the joys
of today, and many more days to come.

The world beyond the door beckoned
more and more. You smiled and said
Sit down a while. And so I did
without a thought.

-- Carter Hill
