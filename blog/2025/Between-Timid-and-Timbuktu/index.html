Between Timid and Timbuktu

Created in November 14, 2025

2025   ·   thoughts   time   Vonnegut   philosophy   ·   thoughts

“The title derived from the fact that all the words between timid and Timbuktu in very small dictionaries relate to time” – Vonnegut.

It is in this very small dictionary that i find myself. Not so many words to say, but looking for the time to say them.

The Time Has Come

It takes time to get organized, let alone energized to write. I’ve thought on and off for many years about putting my personality in a more stable form. My conversation skills continue to hone with every passing beer. People know and refer to me as a talker. In my mind i like to think of myself as a thinker. Now, i think its time to work on my written word as well.

The Glimmer

Early on, in my peak hilarity as a sophomore in high school, i dabbled with the thought of of a podcast. I had a friend named Justin who fueled my ego immensely. He was the kind of boy who would grow into a man who wears an apron in the kitchen with the words “Daddy’s kitchen” displayed in cursive writing. A gift he’d love dearly from his wife. Never giving a second thought to what people would think about it. This silly man found me to be the silliest guy in town. I was already tech savvy at the time, and could setup a stream with a go pro, noodle mic, and a painful overhead light. This amounted to videos on my phone that i can use to this day to embarrass us both. Nothing is funnier in the world than two sophomore boys to two sophomore boys. After we made a handful of videos, Junior year came around and all hope was lost.

High Production

Since those initial outings, I have recorded various Youtube videos of varying quality during my college years. None worth the space on Google’s hard drives, but they will live on forever for future generations to study. They’ll dissect and discuss “why would he film the back of his head instead of his face?” Some of the smarter ones will pick up on my use of long single shots without any cuts as an artistic choice instead of laziness.

My skills in video production are lacking to say the least. My time to hone them is lacking more. Few things in life would make me happier than being an expert in Blender, Photoshop, or Premiere. Some things that come to mind are:

  • Taking too long to order in a line of people at a counter
  • Taking a photo of a group of people when they ask “can you take a photo of us?”
  • Attempting to pet a dog but they growl at you
  • Stepping in some grass and getting your socks wet
  • Trying to open a letter with your bare hands and ripping it to shreds

There’s so much more in life to do, my video editing skills will have to wait for another day.

Welcome to the Blog

We’ve settled at last to my blog. Just my writing and maybe some photo evidence of life. It’s easy, quick, and I’m giving the AI scraping a little sprinkling of me. Instead of Creole seasoning, call it Carter seasoning. Just a little something to spice things up.

The prompters of the future can wonder how the models think Groundhog Day is one of the greatest movies ever made, or how it can misuse commas and semicolons in a sentence almost constantly. They’ll see these quirks and think of me. Hopefully they love it.
